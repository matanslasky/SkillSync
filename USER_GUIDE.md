## Getting Started

### Registration

1. Navigate to the SkillSync homepage
2. Click **"Sign Up"** in the navigation bar
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Select your primary role
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Verify your email address

### First Login

1. Click **"Sign In"**
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll be greeted with the **Profile Completion Wizard**
4. Complete your profile for better team matching

## Creating Projects

### Start a New Project

1. Navigate to **Dashboard**
2. Click **"Create New Project"** button
3. Fill in project details:

#### Project Information
- **Project Name** - Clear, descriptive name
- **Description** - Detailed project overview
- **Category** - Web App, Mobile App, Game, etc.
- **Tech Stack** - Technologies you'll use

#### Team Requirements
- **Roles Needed** - Select required team roles
- **Team Size** - Maximum team members
- **Project Duration** - Timeline estimate

#### Settings
- **Visibility** - Public (Marketplace) or Private
- **Join Approval** - Auto-accept or manual review

4. Click **"Create Project"**

### Project Dashboard

Your project dashboard provides:
- **Overview** - Project stats and progress
- **Team Members** - Current collaborators
- **Tasks** - Kanban board for task management
- **Milestones** - Track major goals
- **Activity Feed** - Recent project updates

**Option 1: Direct Invite**
1. Go to your project

## Task Management

### Kanban Board

SkillSync uses a Kanban-style board with 4 columns:
1. **To Do** - Planned tasks
2. **In Progress** - Active work
3. **Review** - Pending review
4. **Done** - Completed tasks

### Creating Tasks

1. Click **"Add Task"** in any column
2. Fill in task details:
   - **Title** - Clear, actionable description
   - **Description** - Detailed requirements
   - **Assignee** - Team member responsible
   - **Priority** - Low, Medium, High, Critical
   - **Due Date** - Deadline
   - **Tags** - Categories or labels
   - **Estimated Time** - Hours/days estimate

3. Click **"Create Task"**

### Managing Tasks

**Move Tasks**
- Drag and drop between columns
- Updates automatically sync

**Edit Tasks**
- Click task card to open details
- Update any field
- Add comments
- Attach files

**Task Filters**
- Filter by assignee
- Filter by priority
- Filter by tags
- Search by title

### Milestones

Create milestones for major project goals:
1. Click **"Add Milestone"**
2. Set milestone name and deadline
3. Link related tasks
4. Track progress automatically

## Commitment Score

Your **Commitment Score** reflects reliability and engagement.

### How It's Calculated

Points are awarded for:
- ✅ Completing tasks on time (+10)
- ✅ Early task completion (+5)
- ✅ Regular activity/updates (+3)
- ✅ Helping team members (+5)
- ✅ Quality contributions (+8)

Points are deducted for:
- ❌ Missing deadlines (-10)
- ❌ Leaving projects early (-15)
- ❌ Inactivity for 7+ days (-5)
- ❌ Declining too many invites (-3)

### Score Ranges

| Score | Level | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 90-100 | Excellent | Highly reliable collaborator |
| 75-89 | Good | Consistent and dependable |
| 60-74 | Fair | Room for improvement |
| Below 60 | Needs Work | Focus on commitment |

### Improving Your Score

1. **Set realistic deadlines** - Don't overcommit
2. **Communicate proactively** - Update status regularly
3. **Complete tasks on time** - Prioritize deadlines
4. **Stay active** - Log in regularly
5. **Be a team player** - Help others when possible

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is SkillSync free to use?**
A: Yes! SkillSync is completely free for individuals and small teams.

**Q: How many projects can I join?**
A: You can be active in up to 10 projects simultaneously.

**Q: Can I work on private projects?**
A: Yes, project creators can set projects as private (invite-only).

**Q: What if I need to leave a project?**
A: Navigate to Project Settings → Leave Project. Note: This may affect your Commitment Score.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions).

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Currently web-based. Mobile apps are in our roadmap!

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Click "Report Issue" in settings or email support@skillsync.com

**Q: Can I export my project data?**
A: Yes! Go to Project Settings → Export Data.

### Collaboration Questions

**Q: How do I resolve team conflicts?**
A: Use our built-in mediation features or contact support for serious issues.

**Q: What if a team member isn't contributing?**
A: Project owners can remove inactive members. Document issues first.

**Q: Can I transfer project ownership?**
A: Yes, in Project Settings → Transfer Ownership.

**Q: How does the Synergy Meter work?**
A: It analyzes communication patterns, task completion rates, and collaboration metrics.

### Account Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page. Check your email for reset link.

**Q: Can I change my username?**
A: Yes, once every 30 days in Account Settings.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, each person should have only one account per our Terms of Service.
